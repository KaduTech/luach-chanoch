# Luach Chanoch Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 September 2026

Luach Chanoch is a calendar companion that calculates dates using the calendar convention selected on your device.

## Information we collect

The current version does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. Calendar settings, onboarding completion, display preference, and private journal entries are stored locally on the device.

## Notifications

The app offers optional local calendar reminders for Sabbaths and feast observances. The operating system asks for permission only when you enable them. Reminder preferences and schedules are stored on the device and are not sent to a server.

## Third parties

The current version does not include advertising, analytics, sign-in services, or remote content services. If those are introduced, this policy and store disclosures must be updated before release.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

Material updates to this policy will be published at the public policy URL before they take effect.
